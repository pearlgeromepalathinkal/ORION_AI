Freshness: 0.87

Content:
If external monitors disconnect intermittently via USB-C dock:
1. Unplug USB-C power delivery cable for 15 seconds to reset dock firmware.
2. Update DisplayLink / Thunderbolt dock drivers.
3. Ensure laptop charger is plugged directly into dock power port.
