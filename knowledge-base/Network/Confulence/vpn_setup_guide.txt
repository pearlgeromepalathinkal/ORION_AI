Title: Corporate VPN Setup and Troubleshooting Guide
Source: Confluence
Domain: Network
Content:
To connect to the corporate VPN, use GlobalProtect client with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
If you recently reset or changed your Active Directory or Single Sign-On (SSO) password, GlobalProtect will continue asking for credentials until you refresh your connection profile or clear cached credentials.

Step-by-step resolution for post-password reset VPN connection failure:
1. Open GlobalProtect client.
2. Click on the menu icon and select Settings.
3. Select the active portal address and click Disconnect / Refresh Connection.
4. When prompted, enter your NEW Active Directory password and complete the MFA push on your phone.
5. If issues persist, flush DNS via command prompt: ipconfig /flushdns (Windows) or sudo dscacheutil -flushcache (macOS).
