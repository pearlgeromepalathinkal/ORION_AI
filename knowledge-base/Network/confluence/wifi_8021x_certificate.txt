Freshness: 0.9

Content:
If device disconnects from Corporate-Secure Wi-Fi:
1. Forget 'Corporate-Secure' network in Network Settings.
2. Reconnect and accept the updated RADIUS domain certificate.
3. Authenticate using your domain credentials (user@company.com).
