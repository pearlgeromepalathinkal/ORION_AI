Content:
If IP phone displays 'No LLDP Response' or fails to get voice VLAN:
1. Verify switch port configuration has voice vlan 200 enabled.
2. Check switchport trunk native vlan matches data vlan 100.
3. Power cycle phone by disconnecting PoE cable for 10 seconds.
