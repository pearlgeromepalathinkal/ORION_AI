Title: Internal DNS Name Resolution Failure
Source: Confluence
Domain: Network
Freshness: 0.88
Authority: 0.85

Content:
When internal hostnames fail to resolve:
1. Verify active VPN connection if working remotely.
2. Confirm DNS servers are set to 10.0.0.10 and 10.0.0.11.
3. Restart the Network Location Awareness service (Windows) or mDNSResponder (macOS).
