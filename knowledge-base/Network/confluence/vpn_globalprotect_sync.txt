To resolve GlobalProtect VPN authentication failure after an Active Directory password change:
1. Open GlobalProtect client settings.
2. Disconnect and refresh portal credentials.
3. Enter your updated AD password and confirm MFA push on your phone.
4. If connection fails, flush local DNS cache via ipconfig /flushdns (Windows) or sudo dscacheutil -flushcache (macOS).
