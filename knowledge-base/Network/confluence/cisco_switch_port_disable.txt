Content:
Port error-disabled state occurs when unauthorized MAC address is detected:
1. Contact Network Operations to clear port-security sticky MAC.
2. Run command: shutdown followed by no shutdown on switchport Gi1/0/12.
3. Verify device MAC address is registered in CMDB.
