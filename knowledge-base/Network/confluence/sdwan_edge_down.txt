Title: Branch Office SD-WAN Tunnel Failover
Source: Confluence
Domain: Network
Freshness: 0.93
Authority: 0.95
Reliability: 0.91
Content:
If primary ISP link fails at a branch office:
1. SD-WAN appliance automatically fails over to 5G cellular backup.
2. Bandwidth intensive applications are throttled per QoS policy.
3. ISP ticket is automatically created via automated SNMP trap.
