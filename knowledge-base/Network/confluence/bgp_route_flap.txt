Title: Data Center BGP Routing Stabilization
Source: Confluence
Domain: Network
Freshness: 0.91
Authority: 0.94

Content:
During BGP route flap events affecting WAN links:
1. Traffic automatically reroutes over secondary IPsec tunnel.
2. Monitor route dampening metrics on core routers.
3. Do not manually clear BGP sessions without Network Lead approval.
