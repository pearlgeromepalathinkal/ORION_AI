Active Directory password requirements:
1. Passwords must be at least 16 characters long.
2. Accounts lock out after 5 consecutive failed attempts.
3. Self-service unlock available at https://password.company.com.
4. Passwords sync across VPN, SSO, and email within 15 minutes.
