Content:
Debugging Kubernetes Pod CrashLoopBackOff:
1. Inspect pod logs: kubectl logs <pod-name> --previous.
2. Check events: kubectl describe pod <pod-name>.
3. Verify memory requests and limits in deployment YAML manifest.
